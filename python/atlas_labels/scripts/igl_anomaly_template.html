IGL Anomaly Check

A model of the whole IGL, learned from the five intact controls, describes every 100 µm stretch of IGL ribbon by its width, depth below the EGL, ML thickness, contact with the EGL and the white matter, bend, and NeuN / DAPI / p27 brightness. A stretch is flagged when it is improbable for a control (global test) or differs from the same ribbon 150–450 µm away more than intact ribbons ever do (local test). Ribbon ends are flagged as breaks when the gap model can continue them cheaply. Below are the seven 10x controls the model was not fitted on.
