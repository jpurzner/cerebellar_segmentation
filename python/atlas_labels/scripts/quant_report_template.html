Ezh2 cKO Layer Quantification

Layer thickness and area, stain intensity per layer, and the shape and orientation of the nuclei in the molecular layer, WT against cKO, measured on the new labels. The 2017 litter (10x) and the 2018 litter (20x) are pooled, with litter as a factor in every test.

Genotypes, pixel sizes and pooling

Genotypes are the paper's, unchanged for the revision (analysis/p27_NeuN_Ezh2_cko_2024_1_09.Rmd). 2017 litter (10x): A, B and E cKO; C, D, F and G WT, and the letter is the mouse. 2018 litter (20x): slide position 1–5 is cKO, cKO, WT, cKO, cKO, so the gold s1_2 is cKO. Only positions 2, 4 and 5 of the 20x slides are labelled, so the pooled set here is 3 WT and 6 cKO mice; the paper's 4 WT / 7 cKO needs the 20x wild type (s1_3, s2_3, s3_3) and position 1 labelled.

Pixel sizes: 10x 1.0239 µm/px, 20x 0.5119 µm/px, as in the original pipeline and the notebook's scaling_ratio <- 2. Versions 1–5 of this page used 0.65 µm/px for the 10x, so every 10x length here is 1.575× and every area 2.48× theirs. The 10x labels were built at 0.65 and are rescaled, with each µm threshold of the measurement converted; they were not rebuilt.

Pooling: at the right scale the 2017 cerebella are bigger than the 2018 ones (2017 cKO IGL {{n:IGL_thick_tm_um.ko|.0f}} µm against {{n:IGL_thick_tm_um.k20|.0f}} µm in the 2018 cKO; section area {{n:layers_area_mm2.ko|.1f}} against {{n:layers_area_mm2.k20|.1f}} mm²), so every test is cKO − WT on mouse means with litter as a factor. With no 2018 wild type labelled, the genotype difference still comes from the 2017 litter; the 2018 cKO mice add to the estimate of the spread. The tables also give p without the litter term (inflated by the litter difference) and for the 2017 litter alone.

Pixel size of the 10x images

The 10x panoramas carry no scale. Three checks, against the control specimens and the microscope records:

  • Microscope records. The 2017 .zvi files from the same session are 63x/1.4 oil images through a 0.63× camera adapter on an Axio Imager.M2 with an AxioCam MR3 (6.45 µm pixels). The 2018 20x files show the same microscope, camera and adapter (0.5119 µm/px). Each panorama is exactly 5 × 5 full camera frames. 10x on that set-up: 6.45 / (10 × 0.63) = 1.024 µm/px. Version 1's 0.65 came from reading the 63x file as 40x with no adapter.
  • Nuclei. The 20x sections, blurred and resampled as a 10x would see them, match the real 10x nuclear texture only at 1.0–1.15 µm/px; at 0.65 the 10x nuclei would be about 40 % smaller than the 20x ones.
  • Whole cerebellum against the Allen P7 atlas. The 20x sections fit the atlas at {{n:allen.rel20|.2f}}× its size. At 1.0239 the 10x sections come out {{n:allen.rel10|.2f}}×, WT and cKO alike: the 2017 cerebella are larger, while their nuclei are the same size. That is a litter difference, not a pixel error, and it is why litter is a factor in every test.

Do the labels make the difference?

The labels come from a pipeline that treated the 10x cKO mice as controls, so the thickness was measured a second way from the stains alone, on the same columns. The EGL bottom is where DAPI falls past half way from the section's EGL level to its ML level, and the IGL top is where NeuN rises past half way from ML to IGL, each held for 4 µm. The DAPI cut falls inside the less dense inner EGL, so the stain EGL is thinner in both genotypes. The stains give the ML difference the same direction at about a third of the size, and no EGL difference.

Nuclei in the molecular layer

This redoes segment_ml_nuclei from the old repo on whole sections. DAPI nuclei are segmented with an adaptive threshold and a watershed, kept when they lie inside the final ML label at least 3 µm from the EGL and IGL, and called NeuN+ as before. The angle is between the nucleus's long axis and the line from the nucleus perpendicular to the IGL, taken from the gradient of the distance to the IGL label smoothed over 10 µm. The old code measured it against the rows of a hand-cut crop. Round nuclei have no meaningful axis and come out uniformly oriented (panel H), so the orientation is read from the elongated ones (eccentricity ≥ 0.85), which point radially: within 30° of the IGL perpendicular for {{n:radial_elong.wt|.0%}} of them in WT.

How it was measured

  • Labels: the final grids of the flattening pipeline (EGL repair v10, flatten v11, flat_fix v9; the gold s1_2 is your tracing). The open EGL-label spots from Version 10 (s3_2, s3_5, s2_4, s2_2) are still in these labels.
  • Pixel sizes: 10x 1.0239 µm/px, 20x 0.5119 µm/px. The 10x labels and strips were built at 0.65 µm/px; lengths are multiplied by 1.575, areas by 2.48, and every µm threshold below is applied in true µm.
  • Columns: the flattened strips, columns at right angles to the surface 2 µm apart, sampled every 2 µm (3.15 µm at 10x). A column counts when it is EGL-covered cortex, not a geometry error, reads EGL → ML → IGL from the surface (leading unassigned cells up to 40 µm skipped, gaps up to 4 µm in the EGL and 8 µm in the IGL bridged), and the IGL ends inside it. That keeps 49–72 % of each section's covered surface.
  • Statistics: each mouse is the mean of its sections. Least squares on mouse means, value ~ genotype + litter: the genotype coefficient, its 95 % CI and p (3 WT, 6 cKO mice; 6 residual degrees of freedom). Beside it, Welch's t without the litter term and within the 2017 litter (3 v 3).
  • Nuclei: DAPI minus a 10 µm Gaussian background; foreground above the local 10.7 µm mean by 0.25 robust SD and above 60 % of the ML's Otsu level; holes filled, 0.5 µm opening; watershed with peaks ≥ 2.6 µm apart; kept at 14–120 µm² (true µm at both magnifications), solidity ≥ 0.85, ≥ 80 % inside the ML. NeuN+: ≥ 30 % of the nucleus above the NeuN Otsu level of the ML.

Open questions

  • The 20x wild type: position 3 (s1_3, s2_3, s3_3) and position 1 (s2_1, s3_1) have full .zvi panoramas in ~/Dropbox/images/edu_repeat/p27/ but no labels. Labelling them gives the paper's 4 WT / 7 cKO for the layer measures and lets the 2018 litter contribute to the genotype difference itself.
  • The 10x labels were built at 0.65 µm/px, so the µm thresholds inside the labelling steps (gap bridges, smoothing, minimum sizes) acted 1.575× larger than intended on the 10x. The measurements are rescaled exactly; the labels themselves were not rebuilt.
  • The 2017 cerebella are about 1.25× the Allen P7 atlas and the 2018 ones about 1×. The collection dates or weights of the two litters would tell whether this is age.
  • In the 20x sections the labelled EGL ({{n:EGL_thick_tm_um.k20|.0f}} µm) is more than twice the EGL the DAPI boundary gives ({{n:EGL_stain_thick_tm_um.k20|.0f}} µm); in the 10x set it is about 1.7× as thick ({{n:EGL_thick_tm_um.wt|.0f}} against {{n:EGL_stain_thick_tm_um.wt|.0f}} µm in WT). Part of that is the inner EGL being less dense, but these are also the sections with the open EGL-label spots, so their thick cKO EGL needs a look before it is read as phenotype.
