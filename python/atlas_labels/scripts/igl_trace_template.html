Connecting to saved traces…

  • Trace missing IGL: click along the middle of a stretch of IGL, in order, where it is missing or mislabelled; it is painted as a ribbon of the section's IGL width. Start a new stroke for each separate stretch.
  • Outline missing IGL area: click around the edge of a region that should all be IGL; everything inside the outline is filled.
  • Mark wrong IGL: click along IGL labels that are not IGL, and say in the notes what they are.
  • Cyan outline: the current IGL labels; white rings: where the ribbon ends.
  • EZH2 cKO sections: mark only labelling errors. Where the IGL is genuinely diffuse, thin or broken, outline or trace it as it really is, not as a control would look.
