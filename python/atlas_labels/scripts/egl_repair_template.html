EGL repair from the IGL

Fifth version, after your fourth round of notes. The IGL decides where cortex is; the stains decide where the EGL is: bright nuclei with low NeuN is EGL, high NeuN is IGL. Fissures are cut down the middle of the fused EGL and continued towards the furrow base; EGL is filled in from the stain, or bridged across short gaps, wherever IGL lies below; EGL and IGL labels the stains contradict are swapped. Every column from the line must read EGL, ML, IGL, white matter in that order. New here: p27 and NeuN tell arachnoid and pia (no p27, no NeuN) from the EGL (more p27 than the ML).
