New: a mathematical model of the IGL ribbon. The ribbon is treated as a smooth centre line with a width, sitting at its own depth below the EGL. A missing stretch is predicted as the curve that bends least and stays at that depth, joining the two sides with matching directions. Tested on 180 stretches of 50–500 µm cut out of your intact control ribbons, it puts the centre line a median 5 µm from the truth (the old repair: 15 µm, and it failed on 16 of 90 test gaps); for gaps of 350–500 µm, 83% land within 20 µm (old: 21%).
